
-> We need two bytes for commands codification:
    - first byte is the command type
    - second byte represents the value(e.g. engine speed)

-> Possible commands:
    - turn on/off self stabilitization
    - go forward
    - go backwards
    - turn left
    - turn right


-> Codification:
    - turn on stabilization: o1=0x01, o2= 0x01
    - turn off stabilization: o1=0x01, o2= 0x00

    - go forward: o1=0x02, o2= 0xxx(0;255)
    - go backwards: o1=0x12, o2= 0xxx(-255;-1)

    - turning: o1=0x03, o2= 0xxx(78 - max right; 138 - max left)
